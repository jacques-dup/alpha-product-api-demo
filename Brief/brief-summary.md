## Your challenge

Conceive, build and deploy a new digital experience that meaningfully supports Alpha's mission, participants, churches, teams or global operations. You choose the problem, user, platform, product form, stack and tools.

## Timebox

- You have a 48-hour submission window.
- Maximum expected effort: 12 hours.
- Your start time and deadline will be supplied separately.
- At cutoff, all submitted artifacts are frozen. Make no further code, content, configuration, deployment, documentation or bug-fix changes unless requested by the panel.

## Minimum product

- Fully deployed and accessible to assessors; local-only submissions are not accepted.
- Working authentication with dedicated evaluator and administrator accounts.
- Persistent data and at least one complete end-to-end workflow.
- A mobile-responsive web experience, or a distributable native mobile build.
- A credible, presentable experience aligned with Alpha.

## Submission bundle

- **Live product:** deployment URL and dedicated evaluator and administrator credentials.
- **Source:** read access to the Git repository, the submitted commit SHA or tag, and a ZIP snapshot excluding dependencies and secrets.
- **Walkthrough:** a five-minute video covering the product and important decisions.
- **Executive summary:** a one-page PDF describing the user and problem, solution, architecture, decisions and trade-offs, security, testing, limitations and next steps.
- **Product and technical dossier:** a comprehensive account of the submission, covering every area listed below.
- **Complete process archive:** all evidence of your planning and work — every exercise-related artifact and work record from start to cutoff, including planning, research, notes, sketches, task breakdowns, decision records, drafts, experiments, iterations, test evidence, working logs and work-session histories. Preserve original sequence and timestamps where available. Redact only secrets or third-party data you are not authorized to share, and identify any redactions.
- **Engineering manifesto:** a separate, easy-to-read one-page PDF setting out your vision for the future of engineering at Alpha and what you would bring to it.
- **README:** setup, deployment, service inventory and reproduction instructions.

## Comprehensive dossier: required coverage

- **Product and experience:** purpose, problem, users, user stories, journeys, requirements, scope and key experience decisions.
- **System design:** clear explanations plus architecture, context, component and deployment diagrams; complete database schemas and data model; data flows; APIs and contracts; every integration, dependency and system connection; authentication and access; infrastructure and environments.
- **Quality and security:** testing, observability, security model, threat and risk analysis, data and privacy decisions, and known limitations.
- **Future and production:** roadmap, product and technical vision, remaining work, and the time, effort, people, roles, costs, dependencies and assumptions needed to take the product to production.

## Submission conditions

- Keep the deployment available through the interview and debrief.
- Be ready to demonstrate the product and defend the submission.
- All submitted materials must correspond to the version frozen at cutoff.

## Access and data hygiene

Use only data and credentials you are authorized to share. Do not include production, personal, billing or reusable credentials. Keep secrets out of source control and archives.
